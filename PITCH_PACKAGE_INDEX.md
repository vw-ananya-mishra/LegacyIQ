## **🎓 LESSONS & LEARNINGS**

### **From Building LegacyIQ:**
1. **Agentic AI > Single-Agent AI** - Orchestrating multiple specialized agents produces better results than one large model
2. **Deterministic + AI = Best of Both** - Parser prevents hallucinations; LLM enables innovation
3. **Traceability is Enterprise's #1 Need** - Customers want to know WHY a recommendation was made (source line number)
4. **Speed Creates Economic Viability** - Making legacy modernization 4,100x cheaper changes the business case entirely
5. **Open Tech Stack Matters** - Enterprises avoid vendor lock-in; local fallbacks (Ollama) are critical
